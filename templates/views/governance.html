Data Lineage & Collection: All price observations in this dashboard originate from official Daily Price Index PDF bulletins published by the Department of Agriculture Agribusiness and Marketing Assistance Service (DA-AMAS). The extraction pipeline parses raw tabular bulletins into standardized business records covering retail prices across NCR wet markets.

Cartesian Panel Rectangularization: To support continuous econometric time-series modeling and moving averages, the dataset is structured as a complete rectangular panel grid of {{ kpis.total_dates }} dates × {{ kpis.total_commodities }} commodities ({{ audit_info.total_rows | format_num(0) }} observations). Sporadic single-day unmonitored survey gaps (e.g., non-reporting holidays) are forward-filled using the last verified prevailing market price.

Historical Monitoring Boundary Notice: Pork Pigue (Hind Leg / Ham) was officially decoupled and added to the DA monitoring roster on April 12, 2025. Its preceding 42 dates (March 1 to April 11, 2025) are strictly maintained as unmonitored blanks (NULL) to guarantee that no synthetic or fabricated prices are ever introduced into the analytical database.
